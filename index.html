About

I'm an M.S. candidate in Materials Science & Engineering at Hongik University, working in the Advanced Energy Materials Laboratory. My research designs plastic amorphous halide solid electrolytes for all-solid-state batteries — from scalable, low-energy synthesis routes to the ion-transport physics that make them work.

I'm driven by turning fundamental materials insight into practical, manufacturable energy technology, and I'm always open to research collaboration in the solid-state battery space.

Research Interests

Automated & Accelerated Synthesis

Rapid, scalable, automation-compatible synthesis strategies for amorphous halide electrolytes — enabling high-throughput exploration of composition–processing spaces.

High-throughput

Ion Transport & Defect Chemistry

How local structure, disorder, and point defects govern ion transport and electrochemical reactivity in solid-state materials.
